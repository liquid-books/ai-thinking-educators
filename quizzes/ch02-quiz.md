## Answer Key

**Multiple Choice:**

1. **b** — LLMs predict the most statistically probable next token given prior context. (Section 2.2)
2. **c** — A token is a small unit of text — a word, word fragment, or character. (Section 2.3)
3. **b** — Context rot is the degradation of quality in long AI conversations. (Section 2.8)
4. **b** — Miller's 1956 paper identified 7 ± 2 chunks as working memory capacity. (Section 2.7)
5. **b** — This example includes Role, Context, Task, Constraints, and Format. (Section 2.9)
6. **b** — The flashlight beam represents the context window — only illuminated content is accessible. (Section 2.4)
7. **c** — NotebookLM is grounded exclusively in uploaded sources. (Section 2.13)
8. **c** — Connectivism holds that learning is navigating distributed knowledge networks. (Section 2.10)
9. **b** — Research shows middle-of-context information receives less attention weight. (Section 2.6)
10. **b** — Temperature controls response variability, not speed or safety. (Section 2.2)

**Short Answer Rubric Guidance:**

**11.** Strong responses should reference: (a) the five-component prompt anatomy (Role + Context + Task + Constraints + Format) — the AI cannot know grade level, subject, constraints, or format preferences unless specified; (b) context engineering — the difference between a vague brief and a complete dossier; bonus points for referencing the substitute teacher analogy or few-shot prompting.

**12.** Strong responses should explain: capacity limits in both systems, the weighting of recent vs. earlier information, displacement when overloaded, and the connection to Atkinson & Shiffrin's multi-store model. Practical implications could include: restating key context periodically, starting fresh conversations for new tasks, using Gems for persistent context management, or structuring long tasks in short sessions.

**13.** Gemini = general-purpose AI thinking partner, best for everyday teaching tasks. NotebookLM = source-grounded AI, works only with uploaded documents, eliminates hallucination from outside sources, best for curriculum-based Q&A. AI Studio = advanced prompt laboratory for building custom AI assistants. Task assignments: (a) Gemini — quick, general task; (b) NotebookLM — textbook-grounded responses; (c) AI Studio — custom persistent assistant. Credit partial answers that show understanding of the key distinction.
