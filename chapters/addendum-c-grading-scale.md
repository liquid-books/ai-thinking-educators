## Letter Grade Scale

Final letter grades are assigned based on total points earned out of 240.

| Letter Grade | Points Earned | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| A | 216 – 240 | 90 – 100% |
| B | 192 – 215 | 80 – 89% |
| C | 168 – 191 | 70 – 79% |
| D | 144 – 167 | 60 – 69% |
| F | Below 144 | Below 60% |

:::{admonition} Minimum Passing Grade
:class: warning
A grade of **C or higher** (168 points or above) is typically required to satisfy prerequisites for advanced courses. Students should confirm the minimum passing requirement with their academic advisor or program guide.
:::

## Final Grade Computation

:::{admonition} How Your Grade Is Calculated
:class: note
There are **no weighted categories** in this course. Every point is equal regardless of which component it came from.

**Formula:**

> Final Grade (%) = Total Points Earned ÷ 240 × 100

**Example:** A student who earns 204 points receives a grade of:
204 ÷ 240 × 100 = **85%** → Letter Grade: **B**
:::

Students are encouraged to track their cumulative points throughout the semester using the per-chapter breakdown in this addendum.
